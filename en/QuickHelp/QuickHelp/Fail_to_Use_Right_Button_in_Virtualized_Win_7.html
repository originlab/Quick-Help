5.39 FAQ-740 Why can't I use right button when runing virtualized Win 11 on Mac?

Fail-to-Use-Right-Button-in-Virtualized-Win

Last Update: 9/13/2026

There are some circumstance that you might fail to use the right button when runing virtualized Win 11 on Mac. To troubleshoot the problem, you can run Origin as Administrator by typing OriginPro 2026 (or any other corresponding versions) in the Start Menu search box of Win 11 and pressing Ctrl + Shift + Enter.
